( F )Before you write
What should I include?

What you’re building, who it’s for, any references you like, and a rough budget or deadline if you have one. A link to an existing site or deck helps a lot.

How long does a site take?

A landing page: two to three weeks. A cinematic WebGL site: four to eight weeks depending on how many scenes. A web app: scoped per project, usually in phases so something ships early.

Do you work with agencies and studios?

Yes. I take white-label creative development for studios that need WebGL, motion or AI features on a project.

Who owns the code?

You do. Every project ends with a handover: the repository, the hosting, the domain and a written walkthrough.
