Product engineering

Full products on Next.js, Node and Postgres: auth, billing, workflows, approvals, audit logs, multi-tenant data. The unglamorous parts done properly.

[ 02 ]

Cinematic web

Three.js scenes, GLSL shaders and GSAP timelines choreographed to the scrollbar. Sites that feel like film and still hold 60fps on a laptop.

[ 03 ]

AI that ships

Claude and OpenAI in production: extraction, RAG over private documents, drafting with a human approval step, evals so it stays honest. Local models when privacy demands it.

[ 04 ]

Cloud & infra

Vercel, Railway, Neon, Cloudflare R2, Docker, Kubernetes. Deploy pipelines, observability and the cost discipline that comes from an MSc in Cloud Computing.
